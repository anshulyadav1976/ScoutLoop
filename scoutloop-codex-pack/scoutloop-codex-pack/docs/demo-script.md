# Demo Script

Use this for a 60-90 second judge demo.

## Opening

"ScoutLoop is a self-improving due diligence agent for startup judges, hackathon judges, accelerators, and early-stage investors."

## Step 1 — Input

"I can paste a startup URL, pitch text, or a text-based submission file. I’ll use Hackathon Judge mode here."

Show:

- URL
- pitch text
- mode selector

## Step 2 — Durable workflow

"This is not just a one-shot chatbot response. The evaluation runs as a durable Vercel Workflow. Each step gathers evidence, extracts claims, scores the project, and generates questions."

Show workflow timeline.

## Step 3 — Evidence dashboard

"ScoutLoop uses Bright Data to gather public evidence, then the v0 model via Vercel AI SDK generates a structured dashboard."

Show:

- summary
- competitors
- market sizing
- risks
- scorecard

## Step 4 — Questions

"The most useful output is the question set. It tells judges or investors what they should ask the founder before trusting the pitch."

Show founder questions.

## Step 5 — Mubit learning

"Now I give feedback: the questions are too generic, focus more on technical defensibility and distribution."

Submit feedback.

Show lesson stored.

## Step 6 — Rerun

"On the next run, Mubit injects that operational lesson. The questions become sharper."

Show before/after:

Before:

- Who are your competitors?
- How will you acquire users?

After:

- If OpenAI or Anthropic ships this as a feature, what survives?
- What workflow lock-in appears after 30 days?
- What proprietary execution data improves the product over time?

## Closing

"ScoutLoop is not just an AI report generator. It is an evaluator that learns how to evaluate better."
